FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is my location tracked precisely?

Location is network-based (IP) and approximate — typically accurate to the city level, not GPS-precise. Photo and video evidence remain the primary proof of intrusion.

Does it work without internet?

Local capture (photo, video, deterrence) works offline. Telegram alerts and remote commands need an internet connection.
